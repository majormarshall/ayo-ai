⚙️ One-Time Setup

  1. On your Android phone: Settings → About Phone, tap Build Number 7 times.
  2. Go to Settings → Developer Options → enable USB Debugging.
  3. Connect your phone via USB to your PC once.
  4. Open Command Prompt and run: adb tcpip 5555
  5. Disconnect the USB cable.
  6. Find your phone's IP in Settings → Wi-Fi → (your network).
  7. Enter it above and click Connect.
